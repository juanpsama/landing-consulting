### 2.1 Collection and Use of Information

At Grupo Garpe, we collect only the personal information (such as name, email address, and phone number) that you voluntarily provide to us through our contact channels. This data is used exclusively for the purpose of:

Your information is handled under strict security and encryption protocols. Grupo Garpe guarantees that your personal or business data will never be sold, transferred, or shared with third-party agencies for advertising purposes.
