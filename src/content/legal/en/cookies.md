### 3.1 Use of Cookies

In line with our philosophy of performance and absolute respect for privacy, the Grupo Garpe public website does not currently use analytical, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies. Browsing this platform does not generate traces stored on your device.

### 3.2 Future Updates

Should we implement restricted-access areas (client portals) or transactional integrations requiring technical cookies that are strictly necessary to maintain session security, this policy will be updated, and your consent will be requested in accordance with applicable regulations.
